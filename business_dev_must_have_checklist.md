## 必須カバレッジ追加項目 (上記4列以外で必須エントリ)

各サブフィールドで上記4 + 以下の cross-cutting 概念も投入対象 (合計 7,150 件の構成要素):

### bmi_theory (理論基盤)
- Activity System View (Zott & Amit 2010)
- Resource-Component-Organization-Value (RCOV) — Demil & Lecocq (2010)
- Magic Triangle — St. Gallen (2013)
- Job-to-be-Done framework — Ulwick / Christensen
- Profit Pool concept — Gadiesh & Gilbert (1998)

### bmi_process
- Dynamic Capabilities for BM (Teece 2007, 2010, 2018)
- BM as cognitive map — Tikkanen et al. (2005)
- Business Model Reconfiguration types (Saebi, Lien, Foss 2017)
- BMI in established firms (Achtenhagen et al. 2013)

### digital_platform
- Network effects (direct/indirect/cross-side)
- Multi-homing (Rochet-Tirole, Hagiu)
- Platform envelopment (Eisenmann et al. 2011)
- Tipping markets / winner-take-all
- Governance of platforms (Boudreau & Hagiu 2009)

### alliance
- Transaction cost view (Williamson)
- Resource dependence (Pfeffer & Salancik)
- Real options view (McGrath)
- Alliance portfolio management (Hoffmann)
- Coopetition (Brandenburger & Nalebuff 1996)

### corp_venture
- Strategic / financial CV (Chesbrough 2002 typology)
- New venture division design
- Stage-Gate (Cooper 1990)
- Innovation accounting

### market_entry
- Market entry mode choice (Anderson & Gatignon 1986)
- Born globals (Knight & Cavusgil 2004)
- BoP / inclusive markets
- Emerging market institutional voids (Khanna & Palepu)

### open_innov
- Outside-in / Inside-out
- Crowdsourcing (Howe 2006)
- Innovation intermediaries (NineSigma, InnoCentive)
- Open innovation 2.0 (Curley & Salmelin)
- IP licensing strategy

### lean_startup
- Customer development (Blank)
- MVP / Riskiest Assumption Test
- Validated learning
- Pivot types (zoom-in, zoom-out, customer segment, etc.)
- Innovation accounting metrics

### disruptive
- Sustaining vs disruptive innovation
- Performance trajectories / S-curves
- Modularity (Baldwin & Clark 2000)
- Industry foresight (Hamel & Prahalad 1994)
- Wave 4 / Wave 5 disruption

### subscription_saas
- Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Net Dollar Retention / Net Revenue Retention
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Churn analysis (logo / revenue)
- Product-led growth (Bush 2019)

### sustainable_bm
- Triple Bottom Line (Elkington 1994)
- Circular economy (Ellen MacArthur Foundation 2013)
- Industrial symbiosis
- Regenerative business
- Shared Value (Porter & Kramer 2011)

### channel_gtm
- Channel design pyramid
- Direct vs indirect mix
- Channel conflict / cannibalization
- Omnichannel orchestration
- Reseller programs

### b2b_enterprise
- Account-Based Marketing (ABM)
- Solution selling
- Value-based selling
- Customer success vs account management
- Sales enablement / RevOps

### ma_corp_dev
- Strategic fit vs cultural fit
- Post-Merger Integration (PMI) types
- Acquihire pattern
- Spin-offs / divestments
- Corporate venture capital (CVC)
